## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for reviewing pull requests, triaging issues, and
releasing new versions of `sglang-kunlun`. They may delegate these duties to
other contributors as needed.

To be added as a maintainer, a contributor should be an active participant in
the project: reviewing code, triaging issues, and contributing substantial
patches over a sustained period. The final decision is made by the existing
maintainers.
